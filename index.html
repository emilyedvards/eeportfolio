Ripple by ee Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

Ripple by ee is a Chrome extension that creates temporary water ripple effects on webpages.

Information Ripple by ee accesses

When you activate Ripple by ee, the extension temporarily captures the visible portion of your active browser tab. This image is used solely to render the ripple effect on your screen.

Ripple by ee also uses the location of your click or drag to determine where the visual effect should appear.

How information is used

All processing happens locally in your browser.

Ripple by ee does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share your browsing data, webpage content, screenshots, or interactions with any third party.

The temporary screenshot used to create the ripple effect is discarded after the effect is complete.

Analytics and tracking

Ripple by ee does not use analytics, advertising, tracking technologies, or third-party data collection services.

Data storage

Ripple by ee does not maintain user accounts or store personal data.

Changes

If Ripple by ee's data practices change in the future, this privacy policy will be updated to reflect those changes.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be directed to the developer through the contact information provided on Ripple by ee's Chrome Web Store listing.
